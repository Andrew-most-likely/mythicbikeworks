For Cane Creek, we are the only certified suspension service center on the East Coast.

Signs it's time for service

  • Oil on the stanchions or shock body
  • Harsh over small bumps, or sticky at the top of the travel
  • Knocking, clunking or a top-out thud
  • More than 50 riding hours since the last lower service
