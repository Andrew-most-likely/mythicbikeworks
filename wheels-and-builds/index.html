Questions

Common questions

Can you build a wheelset to my spec?

Yes. We pick the hubs, rims, spokes and nipples with you to match your riding, from carbon race wheels to downhill.

Which spokes do you use?

Sapim and DT Swiss, in many profiles and weights.

Can you order a part that isn't carried locally?

Yes. Special orders are welcome. Ask about a part and we'll track it down.

Do you build bikes for kids?

Yes. From a light, budget race bike for your kid to a top-shelf dream build, no project is too small or too big.

How much does a custom build cost?

Every build is quoted. Tell us about it and we'll put together a plan and a price.
