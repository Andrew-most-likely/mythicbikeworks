Every brand

All brands, one bench

We service forks and rear shocks from every major brand, including Fox, RockShox, Marzocchi and Cane Creek.

For Cane Creek, we are the only certified suspension service center on the East Coast.

Signs it's time for service

  • Oil on the stanchions or shock body
  • Harsh over small bumps, or sticky at the top of the travel
  • Knocking, clunking or a top-out thud
  • More than 50 riding hours since the last lower service

Mail-in

How mail-in service works

Riders ship to us from Massachusetts, Connecticut, New York, New Jersey, Pennsylvania, New Hampshire, Vermont, Maine and beyond.

  1. Send us a message

    Tell us the fork or shock model and what it's doing. Choose Shipping it in on the form.

  2. Get a quote and instructions

    We reply with the price and how to pack and ship it.

  3. Ship it to Peace Dale, RI

    Send just the fork or shock unless we ask for more.

  4. Serviced and shipped back

    We do the work in-house and send it home, ready to bolt on.

Questions

Common questions

How often should I service my fork and shock?

A basic lower service about every 50 riding hours and a full overhaul about every 200 hours is a good rule. Your manufacturer's manual has the exact interval.

Do you service Fox and RockShox?

Yes. We service forks and shocks from every major brand, including Fox, RockShox, Marzocchi and Cane Creek.

Can I mail my fork or shock to you?

Yes. Send us a message with the model and what it's doing, and we'll reply with a quote and shipping instructions.

Do I need to send the whole bike?

Usually just the fork or shock. We'll confirm when we reply. Linkage bearing work needs the frame.

How long does it take?

It depends on parts and the season. We give you a turnaround estimate when you get in touch.
