Why us

Factory-level Cane Creek service on the East Coast

Cane Creek has limited service at its own factory to warranty work and sends riders to its certified service centers (Cane Creek). On the East Coast, that service center is us.

We service Cane Creek forks and shocks in-house, including the Helm fork and the Kitsuma, DB Air and DB Coil shocks. Not sure what you have? Send us the model name printed on it and we'll tell you.

As a Cane Creek VIP dealer we can also order Cane Creek parts and upgrades. Ask about a part.

Riding another brand? We service forks and shocks from every major brand.

Questions

Common questions

Does Cane Creek still service suspension at the factory?

Cane Creek says factory service is currently limited to warranty work, and it directs riders to certified service centers. Mythic Bike Works is the only one on the East Coast. Cane Creek suspension service.

Which Cane Creek products do you service?

Cane Creek forks and shocks, including the Helm fork and the Kitsuma, DB Air and DB Coil shocks. Send us the model name if you're unsure.

Can I mail my Cane Creek shock or fork to you?

Yes. Send us a message first and we'll reply with a quote and shipping instructions.

How long does service take?

It depends on parts and the season. We give you a turnaround estimate when you get in touch.

Do you only work on Cane Creek?

No. We service forks and shocks from every major brand, including Fox, RockShox and Marzocchi. See suspension service.
